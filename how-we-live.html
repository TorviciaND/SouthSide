How we live

Faith in action, every day.

From how we govern ourselves to how we serve our neighbors — this is how Southside lives out its values.

Governance & structure

How we organize and care for one another.

Southside is locally governed and congregationally led. These teams keep our community connected, coordinated, and cared for.

Pastors & elders

A team of five fulfilling specific roles in worship and care, building networks of support for people experiencing illness or crisis.

Church board

Together with our congregational chair, secretary, and treasurer, team leaders and pastors meet to articulate vision and serve as a coordinating hub.

Inner life

Coordinates worship, faith formation, and social functions. We offer quest faith formation for families, mini-retreats 4–6 times a year, and monthly movie nights exploring faith and culture.

Stewardship life

Coordinates volunteers, communication, financial management, and property management and maintenance.

Outer life

Coordinates Southside's relationships with sister churches, ecumenical relations, monthly mission projects, and ongoing outreach and advocacy in the wider community.

Community programs

How we serve our neighbors and the world.

These are the hands-on ministries where our faith gets lived out loud — locally, regionally, and globally.

Recycling

We recycle a wide variety of items including single stream, bottle caps, styrofoam, athletic shoes, metals, and batteries — treating our property as a microcosm of the ecosphere.

Rummage sale

Our annual sale each May and our ongoing Marketplace auction — a beloved community tradition that brings neighbors together and supports our mission.

Healing grief

Six-week groups where participants tell the story of who they lost, following weekly prompts. Customized to preferred times, focused on sharing rather than instruction.

Uganda education

A long-term mission partnership with SITEAW to educate girls in rural villages in Uganda — one of our most meaningful expressions of global neighbor love.
