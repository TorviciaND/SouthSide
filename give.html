A word about giving

Giving is personal.

We believe giving is first and foremost a conversation between you and God — not a transaction, not an obligation, and never a measure of your worth or welcome here.

Whatever you give — of your time, your presence, your resources — it matters. The amount is between you and God. We are simply grateful for every expression of generosity, in whatever form it takes.

🙏

Prefer to give in person?

An offering is collected during our Sunday worship service at 10:00 a.m. You are always welcome to give there — or simply to show up, which is a gift in itself.
